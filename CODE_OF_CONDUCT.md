# Code of Conduct

meshwright adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Its full text applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

We want meshwright to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private
  information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability and conduct reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by a
direct message to the maintainer, [@stritti](https://github.com/stritti). All reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers act according to the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
